Pipes for the Pipers: Our 2026-27 fundraiser

The Shorecrest Pipers Club is raising $6,000 this winter to buy three new sets of Great Highland Bagpipes and other club equipment. A full set of pipes is a serious investment, and each set the club owns means one more student can make the move from the practice chanter to full pipes. This is a great way to support music education even for students not enrolled in music classes. Help us sustain and grow the 60-year tradition of bagpipes at Shorecrest by donating today!

Raised so far: $1,010 of $6,000

We update the tartan as donations come in. Watch it climb.

Donate online
Donations are processed by Shorecrest Boosters Club securely through Zeffy. Prefer to give in person? Find us at events in Shoreline all December. We accept cash and checks made out to "Shorecrest Boosters Club" (write "for Pipers Club" on the endorsement panel).

Thank-you gifts

For donations over $50.00 you will receive a thank-you designed by our students: a set of Pipers Club holiday cards, and for Donations over $100 cards and an official Pipers Club T-shirt.

Where you can hear us this holiday season

You can donate off QR codes and the Shorecrest Boosters Club at various events this winter: Spree Trees, December 4–6. Our pipers will be in their club T-shirts with a donation sign at the ready. Will add a complete schedule soon, but find us—weather and finals permitting—at holiday happenings around Shoreline through the month, and listen for Christmas carols arranged for the pipes: the club is learning them right now! There may be lights on the drone cords. There will almost certainly be Santa hats.

Why pipes, why now?

Pipers Club restarted in 2024 under award-winning instructor Alexa Boss, and its newest members are working their way toward full pipes faster than the club's existing stock of instruments can keep up with. Three new sets of pipes means three more students standing on the steps, on the parade route, and at the front of the Highlander Marching Band, and Pipers Club may be able to perform at school sports and other events beyond football in the future.
