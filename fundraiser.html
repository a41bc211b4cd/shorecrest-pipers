Our 2026-27 fundraiser: Got Pipes?

The Shorecrest Pipers Club is raising $6,000 this winter to buy three new sets of Great Highland Bagpipes and equipment for our rapidly growing program, to keep it free for anyone who wants to take part. A full set of pipes is a serious investment, and each set the club owns means one more student can make the move from the practice chanter to full pipes for free. This is a great way to support music education at Shorecrest, even for students not enrolled in music classes. Help us sustain and grow the 60-year tradition of bagpipes at Shorecrest by donating today!

You can also donate cash or via app using Zeffy QR codes at our various appearances this winter. Listen for Christmas carols arranged for the pipes: the club is learning them right now! There may be lights on the drone cords! There will almost certainly be Santa hats!

Why pipes, why now?

It's a good problem to have: Pipers Club restarted in 2024 under award-winning instructor Alexa Boss, and its newest members are working their way toward full pipes faster than the club's existing stock of instruments can keep up with! Three new sets of pipes means three more students standing on the steps, on the parade route, and at the front of the Highlander Marching Band, and SC Pipers Club may be able to perform at school sports and other events beyond football in the future.
