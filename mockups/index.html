Pick a home page hero

These are three mockups of the opening section of the home page. Each one is meant to let a first-time visitor on a phone see who we are, feel the tradition, and see how to help with the $6,000 campaign for three new sets of pipes, all within about five seconds. Open each one on your phone, then tell us which you prefer. Nothing here changes the live home page.

A. Full-bleed photo

The photo of the current club at Shoreline Stadium at night fills the whole opening screen, with the headline, the tartan progress bar and the two buttons sitting at the bottom over a dark fade. It is the most dramatic and the most emotional of the three: real, current students make the ask, and the headline says why. The trade-off is that the text sits over the photo, so it leans on the dark fade at the bottom.

Preview option A

B. Split layout

A parade photo of the pipers in Ireland takes one side (on a phone, the top), and the ask takes the other: the big "$6,000" figure, the progress bar and the buttons on a solid green panel. It is the clearest about the money, and the text never sits on top of a photo, so it is easy to read. It shows the pipers in full uniform, but it is a little quieter than A.

Preview option B

C. Tartan band

A bold band of Shorecrest tartan with very large "Since 1961" type, the ask underneath, and a smaller framed 1960s colour photo of pipers and Highland lassies with the progress bar and buttons. It puts the tradition first and is the most distinctive and "Scottish" of the three. The photo is smaller here, so it leans on the type and tartan rather than on the picture.

Preview option C
