Pick a home page hero

These are three mockups of the opening section of the home page. Each one is meant to let a first-time visitor on a phone see who we are, feel the tradition, and see how to help with the $6,000 campaign for three new sets of pipes, all within about five seconds. Open each one on your phone, then tell us which you prefer. Nothing here changes the live home page.

A. Full-bleed photo

The sunset piper photo fills the whole opening screen, with the headline, the tartan progress bar and the two buttons sitting at the bottom over a dark fade. It is the most dramatic and the most emotional of the three: the photo does the work of the tradition and the headline makes the ask. The trade-off is that the text sits on a photo, so it depends on the picture staying dark enough at the bottom.

Preview option A

B. Split layout

The photo of the band leading the team onto the field takes one side (on a phone, the top), and the ask takes the other: the big "$6,000" figure, the progress bar and the buttons on a solid green panel. It is the clearest about the money, and the text never sits on top of a photo, so it is easy to read. It shows the students in uniform, but it is a little quieter than A.

Preview option B

C. Tartan band

A bold band of Shorecrest tartan with very large "Since 1961" type, the ask underneath, and a smaller photo of the drummers with the progress bar and buttons. It puts the tradition first and is the most distinctive and "Scottish" of the three. The photo is smaller here, so it leans on the type and tartan rather than on the picture.

Preview option C

The progress bar in all three reads from one shared file, assets/js/campaign.js, which also drives the fundraiser page thermometer.
